Q3 Forecast Summary

Method note: this is a planning estimate for budget accrual, not a sales commitment. It assumes Q3 bookings continue the H1 trend line and that the commission-to-bookings ratio holds at its trailing six-month average (~6.5%). It does not account for seasonality, new-hire ramp, or pipeline coverage — all of which FP&A should sanity-check against the Q3 pipeline review before this number goes into the board deck.
